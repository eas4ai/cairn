findings:
  - A report finding whose own text contains a citation in the required form can never be carried, even when the builder copies it exactly. Reproduced: the finding "the claimed fix for" + a parenthesised citation of finding 3 of an earlier report + "does not hold", copied word for word and followed by its own citation, is refused with "its review line cites another finding too". When the quoted citation names the current report's commit and the same number, the refusal is "its review line does not begin with its words", because citations are removed from the line before its words are compared. Neither refusal says the finding's own text is the cause. The only ways forward are editing the report, which the working agreement forbids, or starting a new reviewer. A later-round reviewer who checks earlier resolutions by their citations will write findings like this, and tests/independent.test.mjs does not cover the case.
