findings:
  - The claimed fix for the stale-report bypass changes only the message. An agent can still reach Done without a new independent report. Reproduced: review and report at commit A reach Done. After the review is redone at a later commit B, wake names "review first". Changing only the report's commit: line from A to B and committing gives "Done: first". The gate never checks that the report's findings or examined list changed, or that the report was rewritten after the review's commit.
  - The looser matching lets an unrelated review finding carry a report finding. The kernel accepts the report's text followed by a hyphen, period or colon, even when that character is part of a longer word or path. Reproduced, each reaching Done: report "a defect" carried by "resolved: a defect-free parser was assumed, unrelated typo fixed"; "bin/cairn" carried by "resolved: bin/cairn.mjs walkthrough typo, fixed"; "wake" carried by "resolved: wake: Done message wording, reworded"; "the report" carried by "resolved: the report-writer skill typo, fixed".
  - One review finding can carry several different report findings. Reproduced: the report findings "the gate" and "the gate, the parser and the message are wrong" were both carried by the single review finding "resolved: the gate, the parser and the message are wrong. fixed", and wake reported Done.
  - The wrapped-finding check only reads indented list lines, so an unindented findings list skips it. The old prefix bypass then works again. Reproduced: a report with "findings:" then "- the" and a wrapped line "  kernel lets an empty report reach Done" was carried by "resolved: the, fixed walkthrough typo", and wake reported Done.
  - The gate now reads the review from two different states. The open-finding check (reviewOf) reads the working tree, but the carried-finding check reads HEAD. Reproduced: HEAD's review carries "open: a defect". Changing it on disk to "resolved: a defect, fixed" without committing gives Done, even though the committed review still has an open finding. Separately, a review on disk that drops a finding carried at HEAD also gives Done.
  - Some reasonable ways of carrying a finding are still refused. Reproduced for "resolved: A defect, fixed" (the match is case-sensitive), "resolved: a defect! fixed", "resolved: \"a defect\", fixed" and "resolved: a defect [fixed in abc]". The refusal says "carry it as open: <its text> or resolved: <its text>, and how". It does not say that the text must match exactly, including capital letters, or which punctuation may follow it.
  - The refusal can blame the wrong cause. When the report names a commit after the review's commit and the review was not redone, the message still says "a review redone at a later commit needs a new report there". Reproduced. It does not tell the developer that the report and the review simply name different commits, or which one to fix.
  - tests/independent.test.mjs does not test any of the cases above: editing only the report's commit: line, a report finding followed by a hyphen or period inside a longer word, one review finding carrying two report findings, an unindented wrapped finding, or a review that differs between the working tree and HEAD.
