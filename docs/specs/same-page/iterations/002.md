## In

Four new commands beside `elaborate` and `verify`:

- `same-page trust <validator>` writes a trust record outside the
  repository, under the developer's home directory (`~/.same-page/trust.yaml`,
  or `$SAME_PAGE_HOME/trust.yaml`), binding the repository, the
  validator name, and the validator definition digest, with the actor
  from git config and a timestamp. Nothing inside the repository can
  grant trust.
- `same-page run [validator...]` executes validator definitions from
  `.same-page/validators/<name>.yaml` as argv, no shell unless the
  definition declares `shell: true`, only under an execution trust
  context: a trust record covering the validator's current digest, or
  `--as-developer`, which records explicit developer invocation with
  the actor. It writes one evidence record per obligation that lists
  the validator, under `.same-page/evidence/<REQ-ID>/`, with the six
  axes, the snapshot identity, the execution trust context, the
  validator digest, the result (pass, fail, error), and the captured
  output. A validator no obligation lists produces no record.
- `same-page attest <REQ-ID>` writes a manual evidence record: named
  human actor, timestamp, snapshot, description, coarse bindings
  (paths), expiry, whether the falsifier was addressed, or
  `--inspection-only` for method `inspected`.
- `same-page acknowledge <REQ-ID>` records the developer's
  acknowledgment of a disproof-clearing revision, after which
  `elaborate` regenerates the obligation.

Requirements, by section of the engine spec:

- Trust model, ENG-001, ENG-003 through ENG-008 -> `verify` says
  `SUFFICIENT` under a named policy and never "proven"; `run` executes
  only defined validators; every record carries its snapshot, binding
  basis, and sensitivity; freshness and sufficiency are never claimed
  past the recorded boundary; unknown freshness is `BLOCKED`.
- Obligation, ENG-017 -> after implementation an obligation carries
  its validators list with attestation (actor, actor type, timestamp,
  snapshot, developer-confirmed) and its boundary (`repository`).
- Evidence record, ENG-026 through ENG-031, ENG-034, ENG-038 through
  ENG-040, ENG-043 through ENG-045 -> the record shape, its closed
  value sets, attested binding fields, no ranking of methods,
  assumptions carried and shown, never absorbed.
- Source snapshot identity, ENG-046 through ENG-050 -> `git:<sha>` for
  a clean tree, `workspace:<digest>` over HEAD and every dirty or
  untracked tracked-path input otherwise; workspace evidence never
  reported for a commit.
- Adapter capability and trust, ENG-051, ENG-052, ENG-053 through
  ENG-062, ENG-064, ENG-065, ENG-067 -> a capability registry in code
  with two adapters, `command` (no capabilities) and `manual`; a
  validator's output never sets a trust axis; extra payload fields
  grant nothing; the four trust contexts; trust records outside the
  repository, bound to identity and digest; the drift gate spawns
  nothing.
- Policy evaluation, ENG-080 through ENG-088 -> one verdict in the
  order `FAILING`, `BLOCKED`, `INSUFFICIENT`, `SUFFICIENT`; a current
  failing result dominates any profile; a missing precondition
  (unknown freshness, validator error, digest mismatch) is `BLOCKED`
  with its reason; partial evidence is shown, never discarded.
- Downgrade handling, ENG-101 through ENG-105 -> each obligation
  stores the clauses its profile required at elaboration; a policy
  edit that requires less is a downgrade finding showing old, new, and
  effect, evaluated under the old clauses until
  `same-page policy confirm` (the developer) accepts it; the skills
  record the acceptance in Decisions and revisions (ENG-104).
- Revision handling, ENG-110 through ENG-120 -> a current failing
  result records a standing disproof under `.same-page/evidence/`; a
  digest change on an obligation with one raises the disproof-clearing
  finding with the prior text, prior falsifier, prior verdict,
  snapshot, counterexample reference, proposed revision, and reason;
  `elaborate` refuses to regenerate until `acknowledge`; the prior
  disproof stays; the skills log the acknowledgment (ENG-116); nothing
  bound to the prior digests is current for the revised obligation.
- Validators, ENG-161 through ENG-165 -> definition shape (name,
  method, argv command, cwd, optional `shell: true`); argv execution;
  trust required with or without a shell.
- Manual evidence, ENG-180 through ENG-183 -> the record, its six
  fields, expiry enforced in evaluation, invalidated by freshness.
- Obligation lifecycle, ENG-209, ENG-210 -> bindings attach only
  after implementation exists (a bound path absent from the snapshot
  is a finding).
- Developer surfaces, ENG-215, ENG-218, ENG-219 -> `verify` prints,
  per requirement, the verdict, the requirement, the required
  evidence, the evidence present with its method and freshness, the
  freshness, `local @ <snapshot>` as the authority, the boundary, and
  the assumptions; `BLOCKED` states its reason.
- PKG-005 -> a test over both hook registrations (the recon gap
  promoted at the 001 close).

Decisions inside this contract, taken at the 001 close:

- Freshness at L2 is whole-repository: a record is `current` when the
  snapshot identity, the validator digest, and the obligation digests
  are unchanged since it was produced, else `unknown`. The recorded
  boundary is `repository`, dependency provenance `conservative`, and
  every record carries the assumption that the environment is not
  fingerprinted.
- `local` is the only verification authority, stated on every record
  and every `verify` entry.
- Execution trust lives outside the repository from the first
  validator.
