Status: Scope contract

Iterations 001 through 005 are closed; this contract presupposes L1

## In

- Challenge declarations, ENG-170 through ENG-172 -> a validator
  definition carries an optional `challenges:` list of declarations. Each names
  a `mechanism` from the seven the spec lists (mutation,
  fault-injection, negative-fixture, double, counterexample-search,
  adversarial-input, harness), an `artifact` path that exists in the
  snapshot, a `command` (argv), and `from_falsifier` (true or false).
  When `from_falsifier` is true the declaration names the `requirement`
  whose confirmed falsifier the challenge realizes, and the record cites
  that falsifier's digest. A declaration with no artifact, an artifact
  absent from the snapshot, or a mechanism outside the seven is a
  finding and produces no record; no mechanism ranks above another.
- The challenge run, ENG-033 through ENG-037, ENG-173, ENG-174 ->
  `same-page challenge [validator...]` runs declared challenges under
  the same execution trust as `run`. The command realizes the violating
  state and runs the validator, so its exit status is the validator's:
  a nonzero status means the validator noticed, and the record carries
  sensitivity `challenged` with the mechanism, the artifact, the
  falsifier-derived flag, and the falsifier digest. A zero status means
  the validator passed under the violating state: the engine records
  weak sensitivity for that requirement, `verify` reports it, and every
  earlier `challenged` record of that validator counts as
  `unchallenged` with the reason named. No validator output sets the
  sensitivity axis.
- Policy and reporting, ENG-074, ENG-175 -> a profile that requires
  `sensitivity: challenged` is satisfied only by a live challenged
  claim. Every `verify` entry states the sensitivity, and an entry with
  challenged evidence carries the correspondence assumption between the
  requirement sentence and the validator.
- This repository declares a `language-check` validator with a
  falsifier-derived negative-fixture challenge for CONF-016, and its
  policy keeps the project default profile.

## Out

- Ecosystem adapters, backend bindings, adapter-derived closures, and

- Mutation scoring, coverage percentages, or any ranking of mechanisms.
- Any change to the drift gate, the language check's rules, or the
  templates.
